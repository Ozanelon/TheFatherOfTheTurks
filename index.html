The Sun Rising in Thessaloniki (Childhood Years)

Mustafa Kemal Ataturk was born in 1881 in a three-story pink house in Thessaloniki. The young Mustafa, son of Ali Riza Efendi and Zubeyde Hanim, developed a passion for military uniforms at an early age. By entering the Thessaloniki Military School, he took the very first step of his legendary military career.

×

Gallipoli is Impassable!

In 1915, as the Commander of the Anafartalar Group, Mustafa Kemal changed the course of history with his words: "I do not order you to attack, I order you to die!" With the victories he won at Conkbayiri and Anafartalar, he stopped the enemy armies and became known worldwide as the "Hero of Gallipoli."

×

The Torch of Independence

Landing in Samsun on May 19, 1919, aboard the Bandirma Ferry, Mustafa Kemal initiated the Turkish War of Independence. By gathering the public under a single banner through the Amasya, Erzurum, and Sivas Congresses, he organized the national resistance and established the regular army.

×

Long Live the Republic

On October 29, 1923, he determined the government system of the new Turkish state by stating, "Gentlemen, tomorrow we shall proclaim the Republic." The Republic of Turkey was officially declared, and Mustafa Kemal Ataturk was unanimously elected as the first President, launching revolutions to elevate the country to the level of modern civilizations.
